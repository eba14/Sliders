How to Play

Master the art of tile-shifting

Objective

Rearrange the scrambled tiles on the Current Board to match the Goal Board by sliding tiles into the empty space.

Controls

  • Click any tile adjacent to the empty space to slide it
  • Only tiles directly next to the empty space can move
  • Use Tab to navigate, Enter or Space to move

Game Modes

  • 3×3 – 7×7: Single puzzle of the chosen size
  • Progressive: Complete all sizes from 3×3 up to 7×7 in sequence — timer runs continuously

Strategy Tips

  • Plan several moves ahead before acting
  • Complete one row or column at a time
  • Sometimes a "wrong" move is needed to unlock progress
